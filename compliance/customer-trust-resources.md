# Customer Trust Resources

The customer-facing surface of the compliance posture: what we publish, what's gated, and how customers verify our claims. Maintained as a separate page so it can be referenced from sales, customer success, and the public trust portal.

## Status

Placeholder — populated as cloud-hosted launch approaches

## Purpose

When a prospective customer asks _"can we trust you with our data?"_ — this is what we point them to. The page is intentionally narrow: it is the index to the artifacts a customer needs to do their own diligence, not a marketing brochure.

## What we publish publicly

* The compliance posture in plain language (the "what" without proprietary detail)
* Subprocessor list with notification commitments
* Architectural commitments (open source code; same code in both modes; data sovereignty)
* Founding principles (Section 1 of the architecture)
* Status page for cloud-hosted availability
* Public security contact (PGP key, response SLA)
* Coordinated disclosure policy (responsible disclosure terms)

## What customers receive under NDA

* Current SOC 2 Type II report
* ISO 27001 certificate and Statement of Applicability summary
* Penetration test summary (most recent)
* Detailed control matrix
* DPA template
* Incident response process detail
* Architecture deep-dive deck

## What we do not share

* Internal risk register (specific risks with scoring and treatment timelines)
* Raw incident timelines for unrelated customers
* Provenance from other customers' deployments
* Internal employee access logs
* Vulnerability scan raw output
* Internal network diagrams beyond what the architecture documents
* Source materials covered by Oraclous-the-company NDA with subprocessors

## What this page will cover

* **Public trust portal URL** — once it exists; links to public artifacts
* **Vendor due diligence questionnaire (VDDQ) responses** — pre-filled responses to common security questionnaires (CAIQ, SIG, custom)
* **Customer-initiated audit clause** — what customers can request, frequency limits, notice period, scope
* **Sales-enablement compliance one-pager** — single-page summary the AE can send
* **FAQ for procurement and security teams** — common questions with prepared answers
* **Trust review cadence** — when published artifacts are refreshed

## How customers raise compliance questions

* **Pre-sales** — via the assigned account executive
* **Active customers** — via customer success or the dedicated security contact
* **Reported security issues** — via the public security contact (PGP-encrypted)
* **Auditor requests** — coordinated through Legal and Security
