# SOC 2 Programme

**Status:** Placeholder — formal programme work begins Phase 6+

## Trust Service Criteria (TSC) in scope

- **Security** — common criteria; always in scope
- **Availability** — included given customer expectations for cloud-hosted uptime
- **Confidentiality** — included given the platform processes customer-confidential information
- **Processing Integrity** — included given mission-critical execution-fidelity guarantees
- **Privacy** — included given the platform processes personal data on behalf of customers

## Type I vs Type II

- **Type I** — point-in-time attestation of control design
- **Type II** — attestation of operational effectiveness over an observation period (target)
